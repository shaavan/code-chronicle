## Reflection

The recurrence-support rebase had accumulated enough corrections that its own
state became part of the work. Commit 10 received explicit-basetime and
original-start token coverage after its instruction was checked. Commit 11
then conflicted where private recurrence-period arithmetic overlapped the
public API. Keeping the public helper and replacing duplicate flow arithmetic
with `payment_window` preserved the inclusive opening and exclusive closing
semantics.

The harder moment came when the rebase advanced past Commit 12 before its
instruction was available. Returning to it meant preserving Commit 13's
changes while adding the missing boundary coverage for explicit and default
windows, period zero, and later periods. Commit 13 was restored afterward,
although its instruction still needs to be applied.

Commit 14 introduced another conflict, and aborting there left the branch
clean with the stack safely ending at Commit 13. We also settled the
fixup-message format in `agentss.md`, using one repository-local message file
with an `Instruction:` section.

The day felt less like forward movement than recovering the shape of the work
after it briefly got ahead of itself. That recovery still mattered. The rebase
is back at a known boundary, the corrected commits have their intended
coverage, and the next pass starts from a clean branch instead of from a
half-understood conflict.
