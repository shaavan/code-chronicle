# Log 460: When Payment Became State

> 09 September 2026

## What I worked on

- Made successful payment outcomes advance recurrence state only after correlating the payment with its attempt and validating its BOLT 12 invoice.
- Preserved basetime, optional state, paid counter, payment ID, completion status, and replay behavior across first and later payments.
- Added failure handling for expiry, rejection, route failure, abandonment, and unknown outcomes without damaging paid progress or retry windows.
- Added manual later-period payments with boundary checks, checked counters, fresh payment IDs, routing and amount policy, and no skipped periods.
- Built the first crash-recovery pass by rebuilding the payment index and reconciling prepared attempts with LDK's recent-payment list before scheduler startup.

## Reflection

The first successful recurrence payment forced the record to become more than a prepared request. The handler has to find the attempt, validate the standard invoice, establish basetime when needed, preserve it afterward, store the exact optional state, increment from the stored counter, record the successful payment ID, and decide completion from LDK's schedule index. That recurrence update is persisted before the ordinary payment update and user event. Replayed success then has to recognize work already applied instead of advancing the schedule again. The tests followed each edge: first and later payments, invoice-derived basetimes, optional state, completion limits, missing data, partial persistence, and actual amounts.

Failures needed a narrower effect. Expiry, rejection, route failure, abandonment, and unknown outcomes clear the active attempt while leaving paid progress, basetime, and recurrence state intact. Retry remains possible while the window is open; `Missed` starts at the exclusive closing boundary. A failure after recorded success is ignored, as is any ordinary update that would regress a successful payment. This made failure handling fit around durable progress instead of competing with it.

Manual later-period payment became its own path through the same [recurrence payment flow][recurrence-01]. It requires a successful prior payment and no active attempt, checks the next counter with checked arithmetic, uses LDK's recurrence and payment-window helpers, and refuses both early opening and late payment. It carries original start, established basetime, latest optional state, payer note, routing override, and amount policy back through LDK. Once preparation is durable, a fresh payment ID remains returned even if synchronous submission fails. Missed periods cannot be skipped.

Recovery then exposed how much the acceptance boundary depends on restart. The payment index is rebuilt from durable records and attempts are reconciled with LDK's recent-payment list. Matching prepared attempts become submitted attempts. Missing submitted payments are not declared failed before replay processing exists, and unexplained state does not receive an invented replacement attempt. Recovery completion now gates future scheduler startup. The remaining gap is deliberate: missing prepared attempts can be identified for retry, though full resubmission still belongs to the next fixup cycle.
