# Log 466: Two Bounds

> 17 September 2026

## What I worked on

- Analysed payload-bucket sizes for the new Onion Message protocol proposal.
- Considered how padding each message to a bucket can reduce information exposed to size-based analysis.
- Analysed maximum Lightning path lengths, balancing network reachability against long routes that can loop between nodes.
- Recorded preliminary results for both parameters as a basis for the next round of protocol discussion.

## Reflection

The Onion Message proposal is still early enough that small parameters carry much of its shape. Today’s discussion centred on two bounds. Payload buckets decide how much padding messages absorb so their size reveals less. Path limits decide how far a message may travel before the route becomes difficult to reason about or useful as a looping attack surface.

The two questions look separate, but both ask where protection becomes too expensive or too restrictive. Larger buckets hide more variation while consuming more space. Longer paths reach more of the network while giving loops more room to grow. Preliminary analysis gave both conversations something firmer than intuition, even though neither result is ready to be treated as a final protocol value.

That felt like good progress for an early proposal. I have starting points for the bucket and path analyses now, along with a clearer sense of which tradeoffs the next discussion needs to test.
